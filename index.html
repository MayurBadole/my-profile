Campaign platformUpdated Sep 2026

PulseReach

Sends a campaign by email, SMS or WhatsApp to a segment of contacts and tracks delivery, opens and clicks. All three channels share one data model, so adding a fourth is provider code and not a schema change. Messages go through a Redis queue and a separate worker process. Contacts, segments, templates with AI generation, automations, login and unsubscribe handling all work end to end.

Desktop appUpdated Oct 2026

A desktop app for running a bar: a live sales dashboard, point-of-sale ordering, inventory and order history. Data stays on the owner's computer in SQLite, so it works offline.
